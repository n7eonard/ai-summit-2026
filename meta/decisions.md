## Slide 15 carries the field finding, not the borrowed anecdote

Decided 2026-08-24. Q19.

*"Six people who disagreed on what a good deal was"* is not first-hand. It was second-hand
material wearing the clothes of testimony. In a talk about judging your own output, that is
the slide that costs the room.

**Replacement, from the field:** *the expert could not state his own rule twice the same way,
and nobody had noticed.* Slides 13–14 already set it up. The structural claim — alignment did
not compress — still holds; it no longer borrows a scene that didn't happen.

Live deck and any leftover "six people" wording in the thesis, Shift 3, and the published
abstract are a consistency pass, not a re-opening of the call.

## Seven months, not three

Decided 2026-08-24. Q18.

The mission ran January–July 2026. Slide 2 (and the abstract, when it can be updated) say

## Shift 4 dropped — the fourth slot is open

*Freeze the surface so people can build habits* is out. It was the only one of the four that

The deck loses slides 26–27 and gains ~70 seconds. The slot is deliberately left open rather

## The relevance filter, after importing the wrong thing

The private mission retrospective was mined rather than filtered. Its criterion is *what
happened on this mission*; the talk's is *what does deciding-and-building collapsing into one
person do to you*. A large amount of product-operational detail got written in — data-quality
pivots, a convergence engine, evaluation systems, handover planning. All true, none of it
serving the claim. It was cut the same day.

The filter now sits at the top of [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md). Test for anything from that
retrospective: *would this still be true and interesting if the mission had been in a different
industry solving a different problem?*

Consequence: [`../docs/case-study.md`](../docs/case-study.md) is organised as five situations
plus what worked and what didn't — the shape the talk delivers — rather than as a mission
chronicle.

## Anonymisation

The company, its lead, and the domain expert are not named anywhere in this repository. The
material loses nothing: the failure mode carries fine as "an AI proptech startup".

A public, indexed repo attaching critical material to a named company and a findable person is
a different act from saying it once from a stage. Residual exposure remains via the published
abstract, which names the company — a speaker decision, tracked in Q3.

## The four shifts came from the retrospective, not from research

April produced a completely different set — organisational advice for people who control
headcount (rethink your ratios, PMs ship code, invest in alignment rituals, audit the gap).
The current set are findings from an actual retrospective on an actual failure, which is why
they read as failure modes rather than advice.

They could not have existed before August, because the retrospective they come from could not
be written before the mission ended. **Don't re-propose the April set** — one line from it is
worth salvaging and it's in [`spare-parts.md`](spare-parts.md).

## Evidence bar

If a number or a quotation goes on a slide, it gets a row in the claims ledger and a file
carrying its source **and its caveats**. Non-negotiable: the talk argues we've lost the ability
to judge our own output, so loose sourcing would refute it by demonstration.

Corollary, applied throughout: objections are written down rather than hidden. The talk's
credibility comes from the speaker having got things wrong and said so.
