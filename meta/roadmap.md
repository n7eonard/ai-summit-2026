## The talk's claim (unchanged)

When anyone can build, the person who decides what to build becomes the bottleneck.
Execution went up. Our ability to judge our own output went down. That combination is
the whole talk.

24 August of research did **not** change the claim. It confirmed the slogan is already
in circulation (Tomer Cohen; Keith Rabois × Lenny, 12 April 2026: *“the skill is more
like being a CEO now, which is what are we building and why?”*). The talk's edge is not
that sentence. It is having lived the collapse as a failure, not as a career skill.

The one new first-person observation from the day — *the five-person handoff looked like
friction; it was also where alignment happened* — belongs to Q24 / Q26. It is the
speaker's, not Hilton/Ding's.
