# Session details

## Title

**When anyone can build, the person who decides WHAT to build becomes the bottleneck**

## Event

**AI Summit Barcelona 2026** — 22 September 2026
Track: AI for Builders

## Speaker

**Nicolas Léonard**
Experimenta · AI Product Partners — [experimenta.work](https://experimenta.work)

## Abstract (as submitted)

> Engineering output has exploded. At leading AI companies, a five-person team now ships what
> fifteen used to. At Bricks, an AI proptech startup I shipped 10+ production AI agents in 3
> months. Alone with Claude Code as my unfair advantage. But the part of product work that
> actually decides whether you build the right thing — getting six people in a room to agree
> hasn't gotten any faster because it can't.
>
> Alignment lives in Slack threads and kickoff meetings and then evaporates. There's nowhere
> for an LLM to automate it. The result: a compressed PM-to-engineering ratio where the
> bottleneck has quietly moved from "can we build it" to "do we know what to build, and does
> everyone agree."
>
> This talk is about what breaks when that compression hits, what I got wrong shipping solo at
> Bricks, and 4 shifts/changes builders need to make now whether you're a PM, an engineer, or
> a founder watching your team try to figure out who owns what.

## Audience

Builders: PMs, engineers, founders. Mixed seniority, mixed roles — the talk deliberately
addresses all three and says so.

The design assumption is that most of the room is already convinced of the *good* news in
Act 1 and is living the *bad* news in Act 2 without having named it. The talk's job is to
name it.
