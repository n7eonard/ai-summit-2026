## Working rules for this deck

- **One idea per slide. No bullets, no lists.** The deck is a rhythm instrument. Density
  belongs in this repository — that's what it's for, and it's why the last slide can point
  here instead of trying to say everything.
- **Numbers appear on screen, never spoken as a list.** Say what a number *means*; let the
  slide carry the digits.
- **Section dividers are numbered (01–04)** and carry nothing but the act title. They exist
  to let the audience feel the structure, and to give the speaker a breath.
- **Every slide carries the running footer** — title, venue, year, slide number.
